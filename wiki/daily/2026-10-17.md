## Eye lock, eye line, height per model

- Taylor: device distances are measured from the eyes, so the head must
  stay put through stance tweens and the body adjust around it; the eye
  line also drifted between eyes and forehead by model and height; and a
  model switch should take the model's own height.
- Rigged figure now always hangs from the eye contract: the eye point is
  stored in the head bone's frame at build (stature eye line, face front
  less an eighth of head height for depth), and after posture and head
  pitch each frame the whole body is shifted so that point lands exactly
  on the contract in x, y and z. The "Anchor" option (feet/seat vs eyes)
  is gone; the readout shows how far the body was lifted for the lock.
- Eye line: `box.min + 0.936 x height`, the same stature ratio the
  viewer store uses for the standing eye point, instead of 55 percent of
  the head bone's vertex span (which moved with neck skinning and hair).
  `tuning.eyeFraction` still overrides per model when set.
- Height: switching candidates sets the viewer height to the model's
  own (`rig.height` when it is in cm, else the authored 175). First load
  keeps the saved height.
- Taylor, after auditioning more models: the eye alignment is still off
  on some, so the rig needs to show where it thinks the eyes are and let
  the head be adjusted; and the head bobbed moving to and from handheld.
  Added: "Show rig" now draws a cyan dot on the eye point and a magenta
  dot on the head bone; the picker has "Eyes up" and "fwd" sliders per
  candidate (persisted in the audition store as `eyeOffsets`, world cm)
  that move the measured eye point before the lock, so the body shifts
  until the dot sits in the sockets; the readout prints the offsets so
  they can be baked into the manifest tuning later. The handheld plan no
  longer pitches the head toward the hands (`lookAtHands: false`): the
  gaze stays on the display.
- Taylor: in handheld, the hands jumped to the destination when changing
  stance (couch to standing) instead of tweening; and most models clip
  the chair and sofa now. Causes: `heldGripFor` is computed at the
  destination eye height, so both ends of the hand tween used the final
  grip; and the eye lock puts each model's hips wherever its proportions
  land them while the furniture sat at a fixed height. Fixes: the frame
  loop shifts the grip by the live eye point's travel before planning
  the hands; and the rigged figure measures its seat (thigh underside
  under the hip joints, `figure-seat.ts`, the old seat-drop measurement
  restored there) and publishes a `seat` offset through the audition
  store, which `ScenarioProps` applies to the chair and the sofa only.
  The capsule figure leaves it null.
- Taylor asked about buying Almaral's City People Mega-Pack on Fab
  (and a Sketchfab hands pack: not downloadable, Sketchfab Standard,

## Base humanoid sample (Humanoid_Rigged.blend)

- Taylor asked to try `Humanoid_Rigged.blend` from Downloads. Headless
  inspection: a 203-vertex, 402-triangle base mesh in two UV variants
  (overlapping and not), 1.8 m, 28 bones named `Bone`, `Bone.001` to
  `Bone.005` (spine to head), `Shoulder/UpperArm/LowerArm`,
  `UpperFingers/LowerFingers`, `UpperThumb/LowerThumb`, `Hip/UpperLeg/
  LowerLeg/Foot`, suffix `.L/.R`. No hand bones: fingers and thumb hang
  off the forearm. Textures referenced from a "Base Humanoid Mesh"
  folder that was not in the download. Source and license unknown, so
  it went to the gitignored `public/figures/local/humanoid/`.
- Export script inserts `Hand.L/R` at the wrists, re-parents the digits
  to them, moves the forearm weights beyond the wrist onto them, gives
  the mesh a flat skin color, and exports the non-overlapping variant as
  GLB. Added to the local manifest as `local-humanoid-rigged`.
- Two export gotchas on it: the mesh object carried a 0.01 scale over
  cm vertex data, and exporting without applying it left a node scale
  on top of the baked skin data (body shrank to nothing); and the
  missing texture went out as a placeholder image with alpha blending,
  which rendered the body fully transparent. Fixed by applying the
  transform before export and stripping the image nodes, forcing the
  material opaque. The model is in meters, so the height reset falls
  back to the authored 175; its own height is 180.
- Taylor found the source: Enigma Game Dev's "Low Poly Human Mesh -
  Rigged" on itch.io, CC0, a rig of Comp-3 Interactive's CC0 402-tri
  base mesh (itself from Emerald Eel's reference model, credited as a
  courtesy). So it moved out of the private folder into
  `public/figures/audition/enigma/` with CREDITS.md and is a bundled
  candidate (`enigma-humanoid-rigged`); the local manifest entry is gone.
- Taylor bought the City People Mega-Pack. Formats offered: fbx, unity,
  max, obj (no rig), gltf. For the app: the glTF zip (what the loader
  reads, same layout as the free samples); the FBX zip as well for the
  22 animations and any Blender work; the rest not needed.

  Textures folder but not yet selectable.

  palettes (default, variants 1 to 21, Construction 1, Family; the

  persisted and the chosen palette replaces the figure's map, embedded

  goes through Suspense, so the capsule shows for a moment while the
  new texture loads.
