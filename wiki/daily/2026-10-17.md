## Eye lock, eye line, height per model

- Taylor: device distances are measured from the eyes, so the head must
  stay put through stance tweens and the body adjust around it; the eye
  line also drifted between eyes and forehead by model and height; and a
  model switch should take the model's own height.
- Rigged figure now always hangs from the eye contract: the eye point is
  stored in the head bone's frame at build (stature eye line, face front
  less an eighth of head height for depth), and after posture and head
  pitch each frame the whole body is shifted so that point lands exactly
  on the contract in x, y and z. The "Anchor" option (feet/seat vs eyes)
  is gone; the readout shows how far the body was lifted for the lock.
- Eye line: `box.min + 0.936 x height`, the same stature ratio the
  viewer store uses for the standing eye point, instead of 55 percent of
  the head bone's vertex span (which moved with neck skinning and hair).
  `tuning.eyeFraction` still overrides per model when set.
- Height: switching candidates sets the viewer height to the model's
  own (`rig.height` when it is in cm, else the authored 175). First load
  keeps the saved height.
- Taylor, after auditioning more models: the eye alignment is still off
  on some, so the rig needs to show where it thinks the eyes are and let
  the head be adjusted; and the head bobbed moving to and from handheld.
  Added: "Show rig" now draws a cyan dot on the eye point and a magenta
  dot on the head bone; the picker has "Eyes up" and "fwd" sliders per
  candidate (persisted in the audition store as `eyeOffsets`, world cm)

  until the dot sits in the sockets; the readout prints the offsets so
  they can be baked into the manifest tuning later. The handheld plan no

## Five-finger hand graft

- Taylor: Almaral's hands are two-bone mittens ("crab claws"); swap in a
  more detailed low-poly hand. Chosen source: MPFB2 (CC0), through the
  existing headless pipeline (`tools/mpfb/export_hands.py`): the hand is
  cut from the base mesh by skin weights (dominant group picked among
  bone groups only, since MPFB also has membership groups at weight 1),
  decimated to ~450 tris, exported per side in cm with the wrist at the
  origin and 16 bones (hand + 5 x 3). Export gotcha: the mesh must be
  unparented before moving and scaling, or the child gets both twice.
- Runtime graft (`hand-graft.ts`): the asset's wrist bone frame (finger
  direction, palm normal from fingers x thumb) is aligned to the
  figure's hand-bone frame once at build; each frame after the arm IK
  and palm roll, the asset's armature is placed so its wrist lands on
  the figure's hand bone, scaled by height / 175. The figure's hand bone
  is collapsed to 0.001 so the mitten vanishes into the wrist. The hand
  plans now curl the asset's chains (`fingerChains`, four of them, plus
  the thumb) and the grip point comes from its index and thumb roots.
  Skin tone is sampled from the figure's palette texel under its first
  hand-driven vertex. Opt-in per model (`tuning.graftHands`, set for all
  Almaral entries) with a "Five-finger hands" toggle in the picker.
- Picker: two menus (set, then model) replace the flat list, with prev
  and next buttons stepping through every model across sets.
- Taylor on the first graft: the MPFB fingers "flip around wildly" as the
  camera moves, and the decimated five-finger hand looks bad. Cause of
  the flipping: the grafted digit bones were never reset to rest, so
  every rendered frame added another curl (the figure's own bones are
  restored from `rig.rest` each frame). Fixed in `placeGraft`. The hand
  is now a blocky one built by `tools/mpfb/make_hand.py` (boxes, 84
  tris, CC0, ours): palm, thumb, index and a middle-ring-pinky block,
  two bones each, in `public/figures/audition/hands/`. The MPFB hand
  export script stays in tools for later but its GLBs are gone.
- Picker: left and right arrows step models, up and down cycle palette
  variants (captured ahead of the app's media and device arrow hotkeys
  while the dev panel is up); the Outfit menu shows only for the outfit
  look. Open question from Taylor: retire the painted outfit feature.
- Taylor: not wild about the blocky hand; wants the MakeHuman hand at a
  clean low-poly level like the reference image (also: the blocky hand
  rendered inside-out, a box winding bug, moot now). New export: two
  un-subdivide passes instead of collapse decimation keep the quad flow
  (3,162 to 910 tris; three passes gave 636 but lumpy fingers), and the
  ring and pinky weights fold into the middle finger's bones, renamed
  fingers_01..03, so the rig is thumb, index and a three-finger chain.
  The graft code already accepted a fingers_01 chain.
- Hands, third pass: Taylor rejected the MakeHuman reduction too and
  chose OrFenn Schuller's "Low Poly Human Hand" on Sketchfab (CC-BY-4.0,
  downloadable, so it can ship with a credit). New `tools/mpfb/
  rig_hand.py` rigs an unrigged hand from its geometry: welds the glTF's
  split vertices (heat weights need a manifold mesh), finds the wrist as
  the narrowest slice (the model carries a forearm stub), rotates into a
  canonical frame (wrist at origin, fingers +Y, thumb +X, back +Z),
  prints an ASCII silhouette to check the frame, clusters the finger
  tips to place the index and middle chains, places the thumb from the
  most lateral low vertex, binds with Blender's heat weights, and
  mirrors for the right. 1,407 tris per hand, 19 cm.
- Taylor on the first Schuller export: hands massive, on the wrong arm,
  flipped. Two causes: the source mesh is a right hand (the unmirrored
  copy went on the left), and the hand files kept their names so the
  loader and browser caches served the old pair; the hand URLs now carry
  a version query. Then: the wrist cut was too far up (in the palm,
  leaving a gap at the sleeve). The width profile showed the narrowest
  slice at 26 percent of the open-end-to-tip length; the cut now sits
  2 cm below that wrist, with the origin still at the wrist, so the hand
  overlaps the sleeve. Hand length 18 cm, index yawed 10 degrees toward
  the middle finger and baked as rest to close the splay a little.
- Taylor, next pass: the wrist joint is awful, the hand still large, the
  hand bones are missing from the rig overlay, and the hand color should
  follow the figure's look. Done: the rig script now finds all four
  fingers in a band just above the knuckles, binds temporary ring and
  pinky chains, yaws every finger straight (4 degrees of natural spread)
  and bakes that as rest, folds ring and pinky into the middle chain
  (fingers_0N), and tapers the 2 cm stub below the wrist by 35 percent so
  it tucks inside the sleeve. At runtime the hand's scale is the height
  scale nudged so the asset's wrist radius matches the figure's forearm
  end (bind pose, clamped 0.7 to 1.15), shown in the readout as
  "hand x0.00"; the overlay draws the hand bones with a second
  SkeletonHelper; the hand color follows Look (scene gray, outfit skin,
  or the sampled skin).
