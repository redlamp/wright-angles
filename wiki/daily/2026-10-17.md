## Eye lock, eye line, height per model

- Taylor: device distances are measured from the eyes, so the head must
  stay put through stance tweens and the body adjust around it; the eye
  line also drifted between eyes and forehead by model and height; and a
  model switch should take the model's own height.
- Rigged figure now always hangs from the eye contract: the eye point is
  stored in the head bone's frame at build (stature eye line, face front
  less an eighth of head height for depth), and after posture and head
  pitch each frame the whole body is shifted so that point lands exactly
  on the contract in x, y and z. The "Anchor" option (feet/seat vs eyes)
  is gone; the readout shows how far the body was lifted for the lock.
- Eye line: `box.min + 0.936 x height`, the same stature ratio the
  viewer store uses for the standing eye point, instead of 55 percent of
  the head bone's vertex span (which moved with neck skinning and hair).
  `tuning.eyeFraction` still overrides per model when set.
- Height: switching candidates sets the viewer height to the model's
  own (`rig.height` when it is in cm, else the authored 175). First load
  keeps the saved height.
- Taylor, after auditioning more models: the eye alignment is still off
  on some, so the rig needs to show where it thinks the eyes are and let
  the head be adjusted; and the head bobbed moving to and from handheld.
  Added: "Show rig" now draws a cyan dot on the eye point and a magenta
  dot on the head bone; the picker has "Eyes up" and "fwd" sliders per
  candidate (persisted in the audition store as `eyeOffsets`, world cm)
  that move the measured eye point before the lock, so the body shifts
  until the dot sits in the sockets; the readout prints the offsets so
  they can be baked into the manifest tuning later. The handheld plan no
  longer pitches the head toward the hands (`lookAtHands: false`): the
  gaze stays on the display.
- The code-built controller is replaced by the Fireflies gamepad, and
  the Kenney keyboard and mouse by the Fireflies ones (44 cm board
  centered 3 cm left of the figure, mouse under the right hand).

  never ND, Editorial, Sketchfab Free Standard or Fab Standard (no
  redistribution, and a public repo redistributes).
