## Gamepad and peripherals from Sketchfab

- Taylor's first link (Blumbum "Lowpoly Gamepad") is not downloadable
  and Editorial-licensed, so unusable. Searched Sketchfab's API for
  downloadable CC0 / CC-BY gamepads; Taylor then found Fireflies Assets'
  "[VR Ready] Lowpoly Electric Device" (CC-BY-4.0, 27 devices, about
  650 tris each) and downloaded it.
- Split gamepad, PS-style gamepad, keyboard, mouse, phone and tablet out
  with Blender headless into `public/props/fireflies/` (real size in cm,
  origin at the bottom center, palette textures downsampled to 256).
  Gotchas: unparenting drops the glTF root's Y-up rotation unless the
  world matrix is restored, and imported objects are in quaternion
  rotation mode, so `rotation_euler` is ignored until `rotation_mode`
  is set to XYZ.
- Taylor: the pad was face-down (back toward the player), and asked for
  the lime Xbox-style one. The pack's pads lie face-down, so the export
  now gives each a half turn about the front-back axis (face up, grips
  still toward the player, layout stays right-handed); `gamepad.glb` is
  Controller_Yellow.001, with the black one kept as `gamepad-black`.
- Taylor: the pad sat at the wrists, not in the hands. It now anchors to

  of thumb root and finger root, pushed a grip radius off the palm) and

  to the hands. The readout prints the grip spacing: 11.2 cm on Almaral
  man G against 11.0 cm between the pad's grips.
