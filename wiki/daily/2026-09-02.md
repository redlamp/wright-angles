## 3D camera: zoom to cursor, orbit around the click

Taylor asked for wheel zoom on the mouse position and rotation about
the initial click point. `zoomToCursor` covers the first. The second
needed a custom rotation, `components/view3d/pivot-orbit.tsx` over
pure math in `lib/orbit-pivot.ts`: OrbitControls always aims at its
target, so re-targeting on click would jump; instead position and
orientation rotate rigidly about the clicked point and, on release,
the target is placed on the view axis at the pivot's depth so
OrbitControls resumes with nothing to re-aim. Review caught a gap
(the clamp watched the camera's angle about the pivot, OrbitControls
watches the view direction; with an off-axis pivot they diverge and
release could snap) — both are clamped now, with a test. Merged as
`96ab4e0`; 228 tests. Touch rotate is not reimplemented — filed as a
known gap in the component's doc comment. Steps in
[[test-plan-2026-09-02-review-fixes]] §2.16.

Taylor tried it: zoom is right, rotation read as confusing. Added a
pivot marker for the duration of the drag — a screen-facing ring and
dot at the click point, scaled each frame by camera distance so it
stays one size on screen, drawn without depth test so a pivot behind a
panel still shows. Gone on release.

Second try: the world orbited the pivot but the camera kept pointing at
the old target — drei's wrapper calls `controls.update()` every frame
while enabled, and `update()` ends with `lookAt(target)`. OrbitControls
is now disabled for the drag (`63da0db`).

Then Taylor reshaped the gesture (`22d95b1`): plain drag is drei's
orbit again (touch rotate returns with it); **Ctrl+drag** is the
pinned orbit, intercepted in the capture phase so OrbitControls
doesn't take it as a pan. Furniture and the figure pivot at their
bottom-centre — a `userData.orbitPivot = "base"` tag on the root group,
resolved through the model's world bounding box — because orbiting a
cushion corner reads as arbitrary. Device rects still pivot on the hit
point. Test plan §2.16 rewritten.
