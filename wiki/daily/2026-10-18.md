## Carved fingers: the hands that work

- The Opus agent's `C:\workspace\tools\mpfb\carve_fingers.py` (Blender
  4.5 headless) carves four fingers out of each mitten's finger block:
  it finds the block (faces dominated by `Finger1/11`, cut by a flat
  knuckle plane at the boundary's mean depth), deletes it, caps the
  knuckle ring, and grows four tapered two-segment boxes from it (index
  0.92, middle 1.0, ring 0.93, pinky 0.74 of the block length, 12 degree
  curl on the second segment, thickness fitted to the palm). UVs copy
  the block's skin texel; weights are crisp 1.0: index -> `Finger1/11`
  (moved onto the index), the other three -> new `Finger2/21`. Thumb and
  wrist untouched. +72 tris per hand on men, +84 on women. Finger
  direction comes from Finger1 head to Finger11 head (glTF bone tails
  are made up by the importer and point down).
  `verify_carve.py` re-imports and checks bones, weights, winding.
- Batch: 130 of 131 Mega-Pack bodies carved (accessory and tool glTFs
  are skipped by design); `wheelchair_teen` hit a division by zero and
  is with the agent. `install_carved.py` copies the GLBs to
  `public/figures/local/megapack/fingers/<group>/` (gitignored, Fab
  terms) and repoints the manifest.
- Verified in the app on Casual man A, Casual woman A, Boy A and Elder

## Carve v2: strips, not boxes

- Taylor on v1: palms too long, fingers too short (the base joint was
  swallowed by the knuckle plane) and the fingers read as cubes on a
  plate; they should be cut from the mitten's own shape like its thumb
  is. v2 (`carve_fingers.py`, old core kept at
  `out/carve/dev/carve_fingers_boxes.py.bak`): the knuckle plane goes
  through the Finger1 head; the block is bisected lengthwise into four
  strips, ripped along the cuts past the knuckle, the copies pulled
  apart into wedge grooves (0.10 x W/4 at the tip, closed at the
  knuckle, wider on the palm side) and walled; lengths index 0.95,
  middle 1.0, ring 0.95, pinky 0.78 of the middle strip; tip taper 0.75
  thickness, 0.85 width; the strips keep the mitten's blended weights,
  with Finger1/11 weight moved to Finger2/21 on the outer three. Casual
  man A: fingers 8.2 to 8.7 cm (were about 6), +150 tris per hand
  (+142 to +214 across the pack). Two fully closed hands
  (wheelchair_man_ani and wheelchair_teen_ani, left) stay mittens.
- Installed for all 130 bodies and checked in the Hands view, gamepad
  and keyboard poses. Groove width is a constant at the top of the
  script if the fingers need to separate more from the back of the hand.

## Hands, final approach: Taylor's drawings drive the build

- The carve rounds (v2 to v6) and two procedural box-hand builds all
  read as marginal or worse to Taylor. What worked: he drew on
  orthographic wireframe renders of the mitten (`render_mitten.py`,
  contact sheets per mitten family via `compose_sheet.py`): red trims,
  then a green vertex network, then a green silhouette of the whole hand
  from the back plus four hexagonal finger cross-sections.
- `carve_fingers.py` profile `silhouette` (v2): registers the silhouette
  panel to the render (the panel is the back view at scale 1, offset
  +665/+706 px; 1 px = 0.0405 cm), remaps the palm outline onto the
  green palm (fading out before the wrist loop), deletes the finger
  block and grows four separate finger tubes along the green finger
  shapes: six-sided section (flat back, narrower flat palm face), axis
  in the back-of-hand plane continuing the palm's own direction, base
  thickness = the palm's at that knuckle column, rings at the base, 45%
  and 75%, flat tips. The palm's knuckle loop is rebuilt to be the four
  base rings with one small gap quad between neighbors: shared
  vertices, no web triangles, no step. Bones on the base and first-joint
  rings; weights one bone per ring. Other models scale the silhouette by
  knuckle width across and block reach along, thickness from their own
  palm. About +200 tris per hand.
- Lessons: the panel titles "back"/"palm" and "tips"/"wrist" in the
  first sheets were swapped (fixed in `render_mitten.py`); a dev
  scratch folder under the batch output once got installed over real
  models (installer now skips it); a Blender batch loop keeps going
  when its Blender is killed, kill the loop.
- Final batch (11:38): 122 of 130 bodies carry the silhouette fingers
  (v2 junction; six women on the v1 short-web junction), with a
  chamfered tip (last ring set back 0.12 of the finger width, cap at 0.7
  of the section) that Taylor asked for once the shape passed. Eight
  stay mittens: little_girl_A/B/C, night_Female_A, winter_Female_A
  (their knuckle opening touches itself at the thumb web, a figure
  eight the loop builder cannot walk) and the three prop-gripping
  animated variants blind_man_ani, crutches_man_ani, crutches_woman_ani.
  The earlier "32 mittens" was a knuckle-ring sort bug on arched
  knuckles, fixed. Installed and checked in the app on Casual man A,
  Casual woman A, Boy A, Little boy A and Young adult woman A. About
  +208 tris per hand.
