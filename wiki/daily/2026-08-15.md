Rebuilt the figure's head from a sphere into planar masses (faceted
icosahedron cranium, tapered jaw wedge, nose block) and switched the
figure to a flat-shaded lit material with its own hemisphere + key
light — the only lit material in the scene, so the planes actually
read. Eyes stay on the root-local +69 contract.

Distance handles now advertise their drag: hovering the floor node or
the distance text shows the open-hand cursor, dragging shows the fist,
and the text itself became a drag handle sharing the node's handlers.
Verified live: hover sets `grab` on body, resets on leave; drag path is
the node's existing verified logic.
