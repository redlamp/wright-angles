# Low Poly Human Mesh, rigged

- Rig: Enigma Game Dev, "Low Poly Human Mesh - Rigged",
  https://enigma-game-dev.itch.io/low-poly-human-mesh-rigged, CC0
  ("PUBLIC DOMAIN CC0 LICENCE" on the page).
- Mesh: Comp-3 Interactive, "Low Poly Human Mesh",
  https://comp3interactive.itch.io/low-poly-human-mesh, CC0, 402 tris,
  itself an optimized and unwrapped version of Emerald Eel's Human Size
  Reference Model (credited here as a courtesy).
- File: humanoid-rigged.glb, exported 2026-10-17 from Humanoid_Rigged.blend
  (non-overlapping UV variant) with Blender 4.5. Changes: Hand.L/R bones
  inserted at the wrists with the fingers and thumbs re-parented to them
  and the forearm weights beyond the wrist moved onto them; the missing
  texture replaced by a flat skin color; object transform applied.
