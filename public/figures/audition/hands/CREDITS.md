# Hands worn over mitten-handed figures

- hand-left.glb, hand-right.glb: based on "Low Poly Human Hand"
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-human-hand-62851b4ca2fe4503ad447057b461192e)
  by OrFenn Schuller (https://sketchfab.com/orfenn-schuller), licensed
  under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Changes (2026-10-17, `C:\workspace\tools\mpfb\rig_hand.py`): the
  cartoon outline shell removed, split vertices welded, the forearm stub
  cut at the wrist, the mesh rotated so the wrist is at the origin with
  the fingers along +Y, scaled to a 19 cm hand (175 cm body), flat
  shaded with a flat skin material, and rigged: hand, index_01..03,
  fingers_01..03 (middle finger; ring and pinky follow it), thumb_01..03,
  heat-weighted. The right hand is the mirrored left. 1,407 triangles.
