# MPFB2 generated figures

Generated 2026-10-16 with MakeHuman Plugin For Blender 2 (MPFB2 v2.0.18)
in Blender 4.5.14 by `C:\workspace\tools\mpfb\make_figure.py`: base mesh
plus macro targets, game_engine rig, helpers removed, collapse-decimated,
flat shaded, exported as GLB.

(LICENSE.ASSETS.md in the MPFB2 repository) and official unmodified
exports are CC0. No attribution required.

## hand-left.glb, hand-right.glb (2026-10-17)

Five-fingered hands cut from an MPFB2 human (default macro targets,
game_engine rig) by `C:\workspace\tools\mpfb\export_hands.py`: vertices
driven mostly by the hand bone or its fingers, collapse-decimated to
about 450 triangles, flat shaded, flat skin material, exported in
centimeters with the wrist at the origin and the hand bone as the
skeleton root (hand_l, thumb/index/middle/ring/pinky_01..03_l; same
for _r). CC0 like the bodies above. The app wears them over models with
mitten hands (tuning.graftHands).
